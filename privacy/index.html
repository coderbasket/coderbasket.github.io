Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Coder Basket collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Coder Basket is a catalogue of applications, libraries, frameworks, tools, and other developer resources. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to operate the website.

Information you provide

Coder Basket does not require you to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse the catalogue.

If you contact us directly, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, or the contents of your message.

Automatically collected information

When you visit the website, standard technical information may be processed by the website infrastructure or third-party services. This may include information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, referring page, pages visited, and approximate usage information.

2. How We Use Information

Information that is collected may be used to:

  • operate and maintain Coder Basket;
  • understand how the website is used;
  • detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or technical problems;
  • improve website performance and usability;
  • diagnose errors and security issues; and
  • respond to communications submitted by visitors.

We do not intentionally collect personal information that is not reasonably necessary for these purposes.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Coder Basket may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar mechanisms.

These technologies may be used to remember preferences, maintain website functionality, save catalogue selections, or support analytics and advertising services.

For example, Coder Basket may store your selected catalogue section or category in your browser's local storage so that your preference can be restored when you return.

You can generally control or remove cookies and locally stored website data through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand website traffic and improve Coder Basket.

Analytics providers may collect information about visits, page views, device characteristics, approximate geographic information, referring pages, and interactions with the website.

Analytics data is generally used in aggregate or pseudonymous form to understand website performance and usage.

5. Advertising

Coder Basket may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and potentially personalize advertisements according to their own policies.

We do not control the data practices of independent advertising providers. Their processing of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Where required, applicable consent mechanisms may be used before certain advertising or personalization technologies are enabled.

6. Third-Party Services

Coder Basket may rely on third-party providers for services such as hosting, content delivery, analytics, security, advertising, or other infrastructure.

These providers may process technical information as necessary to provide their services.

Examples may include hosting and infrastructure providers, analytics providers, advertising networks, and content delivery services.

Each third-party provider may have its own privacy policy describing how it handles information.

7. External Links

Coder Basket contains links to external websites, including project repositories, application stores, documentation, developer websites, videos, and other resources.

Once you leave Coder Basket, the privacy practices of the external website apply.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external service before providing it with personal information.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the website and information processed through it.

However, no website, transmission over the Internet, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Coder Basket is a general-purpose developer resource website and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Coder Basket, applicable requirements, or the services used by the website.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Coder Basket, you may contact us through the contact method provided on the website.

Important

This Privacy Policy is intended to describe the general privacy practices of Coder Basket. It should be reviewed and updated whenever the website adds or removes services such as analytics, advertising, cookies, accounts, forms, or other data-processing features.
